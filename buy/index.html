Buyer FAQs

What buyers ask most.

Do I have to sign an agreement to tour homes?

Yes. Since August 2024, buyers working with an agent sign a written agreement before touring homes. It spells out the services and how the agent is paid. Saba will walk you through it line by line and answer every question first.

How much do I need for a down payment?

Less than many people think — some loan programs allow as little as 0–3.5% down for qualified buyers. A local lender can show you options; Saba can recommend several to compare.

Do I need to be pre-approved before looking?

It’s strongly recommended. Pre-approval tells you your budget, and sellers expect it with any offer. You can still set up listing alerts and talk strategy before you’re pre-approved.

How do I know which school district a home is in?

District boundaries don’t always follow city lines in Central Ohio. Always verify by exact address with the district or the county auditor — Saba will help you confirm before you make an offer.
