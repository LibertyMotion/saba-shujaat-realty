Seller FAQs

What sellers ask most.

How much is my home worth?

It depends on recent nearby sales, current competition, your home’s condition and updates, and today’s buyer demand. Online estimates can be off by tens of thousands of dollars. Request a free market analysis and Saba will send a personalized price range within 24 hours.

What should I fix before listing?

Usually less than you think. Focus on repairs an inspector would flag, fresh neutral paint where needed, deep cleaning and curb appeal. Saba will walk through your home and prioritize what actually pays off — and what to skip.

Should I sell first or buy first?

It depends on your equity, financing and how much flexibility you need. Options include selling first with a rent-back, buying with a contingency, or bridge financing. We’ll map out the trade-offs for your situation before you commit.

What does it cost to sell?

Typical costs include brokerage commission (always negotiable), title and closing fees, any agreed buyer concessions, and payoff of your mortgage. Your consultation includes an estimated net-proceeds sheet so there are no surprises.

How long does it take to sell a home in Westerville?

Timing depends on price point, condition, location and season. Well-prepared, well-priced homes generally sell fastest. Your market analysis includes current days-on-market data for your neighborhood.
